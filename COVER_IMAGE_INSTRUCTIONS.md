# Cover Image Design Instructions for LikenessGuard Article

## Image Requirements

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 630 pixels (standard social media/article cover size)
**Format:** PNG or JPG
**File Name:** `likenessguard-cover-image.png`

---

## Design Concept

Create an attention-grabbing cover image that conveys the urgency and importance of AI consent enforcement.

### Visual Style
- **Modern and professional** with a tech-forward aesthetic
- **Bold typography** for maximum readability
- **High contrast** colors for eye-catching appeal
- **Clean layout** that works well as a thumbnail

---

## Text Content (EXACT WORDING)

```
Following reports that AI tools have been used to create
sexualized and "undressed" images of women and concerns
raised about child-like imagery, viewers are asking:

What's actually illegal, and who is responsible?
```

---

## Recommended Color Schemes

### Option 1: Bold Red Alert (Recommended)
- **Background:** Deep navy blue (#0A1628) or dark gradient
- **Main text:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Highlight/accent:** Bright red (#FF3B3B) or warning orange (#FF6B35)
- **Question text:** Bright cyan (#00D9FF) or electric blue (#0099FF)

**Why:** Creates urgency and draws attention while maintaining professionalism

### Option 2: Professional Tech
- **Background:** Dark purple to blue gradient (#1A0B2E to #16213E)
- **Main text:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Highlight/accent:** Neon green (#39FF14) or bright yellow (#FFD700)
- **Question text:** Bright yellow (#FFD700)

**Why:** Tech-forward, modern, stands out in feeds

### Option 3: Clean Impact
- **Background:** Solid black (#000000) or very dark gray (#1A1A1A)
- **Main text:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Highlight/accent:** Bright red (#FF0000)
- **Question text:** Bright yellow (#FFFF00) or white with red background

**Why:** Maximum contrast, impossible to miss

---

## Layout Structure

### Top Section (40% of image)
```
Following reports that AI tools have been used to create
sexualized and "undressed" images of women and concerns
raised about child-like imagery, viewers are asking:
```

**Typography:**
- Font: Sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, Roboto, or Arial)
- Size: 28-32px
- Weight: Medium (500-600)
- Line height: 1.4
- Alignment: Left or center
- Color: White

### Bottom Section (30% of image) - THE QUESTION
```
What's actually illegal,
and who is responsible?
```

**Typography:**
- Font: Same as above but BOLD
- Size: 40-48px (LARGER than body text)
- Weight: Bold (700-800)
- Line height: 1.2
- Alignment: Center
- Color: Bright accent color (yellow, cyan, or red)
- Optional: Add subtle glow effect or background highlight box

### Bottom Bar (10% of image) - BRANDING
```
LikenessGuard: Consent Enforcement for AI
```

**Typography:**
- Font: Same sans-serif
- Size: 18-20px
- Weight: Semi-bold (600)
- Color: Light gray (#CCCCCC) or accent color
- Alignment: Center

## Design Tools & Methods

### Method 1: Canva (Easiest)
1. Go to Canva.com
2. Create custom size: 1200 x 630 pixels
3. Choose a dark background or gradient
4. Add text boxes with the content above
5. Use fonts: Montserrat Bold, Inter, or Roboto
6. Apply the color scheme
7. Download as PNG

### Method 2: Figma (Professional)
1. Create new frame: 1200 x 630
2. Add rectangle for background with gradient
3. Add text layers with proper hierarchy
4. Use auto-layout for spacing
5. Export as PNG at 2x resolution

### Method 3: Adobe Express (Quick)
1. Start with social media template
2. Customize dimensions to 1200 x 630
3. Replace text with content above
4. Apply bold colors
5. Download

## Typography Best Practices

### Font Recommendations (in order of preference)
1. **Montserrat** - Modern, bold, highly readable
2. **Inter** - Clean, professional, tech-forward
3. **Roboto** - Reliable, widely available
4. **Poppins** - Friendly yet professional
5. **Arial Black** - Fallback, universally available

### Readability Checklist
- ✅ Text is at least 28px for body content
- ✅ Question text is 40px+ and bold
- ✅ High contrast ratio (4.5:1 minimum)
- ✅ Adequate line spacing (1.4-1.5)
- ✅ No text over busy backgrounds
- ✅ Text readable at thumbnail size (300px wide)

---

## Visual Elements (Optional Enhancements)

### Subtle Background Elements
- Abstract tech patterns (circuits, nodes, connections)
- Subtle gradient overlay
- Geometric shapes in background
- Light particles or dots

### Accent Elements
- Thin line separating sections
- Subtle glow behind question text
- Small icon (shield, lock, or checkmark) near branding
- Corner accent shapes

**IMPORTANT:** Keep these subtle - text must remain the focus

---

## Testing Your Design

Before finalizing, check:

1. **Thumbnail Test:** Shrink to 300px wide - is text still readable?
2. **Contrast Test:** Use WebAIM contrast checker - does it pass?
3. **Mobile Test:** View on phone - does it grab attention?
4. **Print Test:** Print in grayscale - is hierarchy clear?
